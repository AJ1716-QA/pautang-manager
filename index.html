Getting started

A quick guide to keeping your lending records clear and organized.

1. Add a borrower

Open Borrowers, select Add borrower, then enter the borrower’s name and optional contact details. Use notes for helpful reminders only.

2. Record a loan

Open Loans or Overview and choose New loan. Select the borrower, enter the principal, any agreed fixed interest amount, the date lent, and the due date. The app calculates the total due.

3. Record every payment

Open a loan’s details and select Record payment. Enter the amount, date, and payment method. The outstanding balance updates automatically. Keep receipts or proof of transfer separately.

4. Review your lending

Overview shows outstanding balances, active loans, overdue loans, and collections this month. Reports lets you export loan and payment records.

Backup and restore

Download JSON backup regularly and keep a copy somewhere secure. To move records to another device, use Restore JSON backup on that device. Restoring replaces the records currently stored there.

Important data and privacy note

When you are signed in, records are saved in this browser and synced to your private account in the cloud. Keep your password private, sign out on shared devices, and download regular JSON backups. Do not store highly sensitive information. Keep separate agreements and receipts where appropriate.

Backup and restore

Keep a secure copy of your lending records. Download a JSON backup regularly. Restoring replaces the records in your account with the selected backup, so make a backup first.
